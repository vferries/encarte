Encarté keeps your loyalty cards on your phone and shows their barcode at checkout. Nothing else.

• Works fully offline: the app has no Internet permission.
• Scan a card with the camera, pick a screenshot, or type the number.
• Import your cards from FidMe, a Wallet pass or a PDF.
• QR, EAN, UPC, Code 128/39/93, Codabar, ITF, Aztec, Data Matrix and PDF417.
• Full brightness while a card is shown.
• Expiry dates, archive, and contactless payment blocked while a card is shown.
• Photos of the front and back of your cards.
• Favorites, search and sorting.
• Groups, a widget and shortcuts to open a card in one tap.
• Backups in Catima's format, optionally encrypted (AES-256): move to or from Catima at any time.
• Optional lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock.

Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). No ads, no tracking, no account.
