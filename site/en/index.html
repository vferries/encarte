Encarté

Your loyalty cards. Nothing else.

  • Offline. The app has no internet access: it cannot send anything.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sync.
  • Open source. Free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later license.

Encarté keeps your loyalty cards on your phone and shows their barcode at checkout, at full brightness. Scan a card, find it in a second, and back everything up to a Catima-compatible file.
